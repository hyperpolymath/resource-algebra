== Build invariant

* All modules carry `{-# OPTIONS --safe --without-K #-}`; no postulates, no
  holes, no standard library. — PROVEN by `just check-agda` (All.agda).
* Five expected rejections fail at their invalid declaration and for the
  intended reason (`min m n != max m n`, `p != zero`, `suc _ != zero`,
  `max m n != m + n`, `max p₁ (n₁ + p₂) != p₁ + p₂`). — TESTED by
  `scripts/check-agda.sh`, which CI (`proofs.yml`) runs. Until 2026-10-14
  the workflow called a `scripts/check.sh` that does not exist, so no CI
  run had ever checked these proofs; the first green `Agda proofs` run on
  the pinned Agda 2.6.4.3 is what moves this line from local (Agda 2.6.3)
  to CI evidence.

== Morphisms (Morphism.agda)

`Hom A B` preserves 𝟘, 𝟙, ⊕ and ⊗ (what transporting a grade equality
needs). `LaxHom A B` preserves 𝟙 and ⊕, hence is monotone, and maps ⊗ to at
most ⊗ (what transporting a bound needs).

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Claim | Status
| F-1 | Both notions have identities and compose; every strict morphism is lax | PROVEN (`id-hom`, `_∘h_`, `id-lax`, `_∘l_`, `lax`)
| F-2 | `peak : HWM → MaxPlus` is lax: the peak of a sequence never exceeds the sum of the peaks | PROVEN (`peak-lax`)
| F-3 | `peak` is not strict | TESTED (`tests/reject/PeakIsStrict.agda`)
| F-4 | No strict morphism leaves HWM for any algebra with 𝟘 ≢ 𝟙; in particular none to MaxPlus or Loss | PROVEN (`no-strict-from-HWM`, `no-strict-HWM→MaxPlus`, `no-strict-HWM→Loss`)
| F-5 | Exactly two strict morphisms Loss → MaxPlus exist, `zero-test` and `support`, and neither distinguishes `fin 1` from `fin 2` | PROVEN (`zero-test-hom`, `support-hom`, `Loss→MaxPlus`, `Loss→MaxPlus-collapses`, `no-injective-Loss→MaxPlus`)
| F-6 | Exactly one strict morphism MaxPlus → Loss exists, `support′` (resource-algebra#3 sketched this direction as "symmetric" to Loss → MaxPlus; it is not: one map, not two. Its other claim, that every positive grade goes to a single point, holds) | PROVEN (`support′-hom`, `MaxPlus→Loss`)
| F-7 | The identity on the carrier is a lax morphism Loss → MinMax: a bottleneck never exceeds the sum | PROVEN (`loss-bottleneck`)
|===

* K-CUT-LOSS itself. It is not stated here; this repository supplies the
  algebra it will be parametric in. Expectation (DESIGNED): only the core
  record is needed for the equality; `IsOrdered` is needed for the
  K-CUT-WARRANT bound.
* L-2 import direction; M-2 Lean bridge; H-2 supersession by occupancy;
  N-2 typed-wasm adoption.
* Transport of K-CUT-LOSS along a morphism. F-1..F-7 supply the maps and
  say which exist; no theorem here states that a strict morphism carries a
  cut-loss equality, or a lax one a cut-loss bound. That theorem belongs
  with K-CUT-LOSS (choreographic-types), and is OPEN.
* Morphisms out of or into MinMax other than `loss-bottleneck` are not
  classified.
