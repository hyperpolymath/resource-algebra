== Agda leg: invocation and receipt

* *Invocation.* `scripts/check-agda.sh` runs `agda --safe --without-K
--cubical-compatible -Werror --no-libraries --ignore-interfaces
--double-check -i src` over `src/ResourceAlgebra/All.agda`, the unit tests
(`tests/unit/AllTests.agda`) and the eight expected rejections. A rejection
must fail at its invalid declaration with the message its `-- EXPECT:` line
names. A refusal with any other message fails the gate, and so does any
warning.
* *Import cone.* Only `src/ResourceAlgebra/`: no standard library and no
other library. There is no `postulate` (refused by `--safe`), no hole and no
`TERMINATING` or similar pragma.
* *Receipt.* CI (`proofs.yml`) installs Agda 2.6.4.3, asserts the version
and checks the proofs. It then writes a receipt to the run summary with the
source commit, `agda --version`, the package version and its SHA256 from
Debian's signed index, the digest of the installed binary, the flags read
from the script, and the import cone. Installing Agda from Debian rather
than Guix is a §6 deviation, recorded in `proofs.yml`; its state is `open`.
* *History.* Until 2026-11-09 the gate ran `--safe --without-K
--no-libraries --double-check`. That profile passed in CI from 2026-10-14
(resource-algebra#4). Under §2.1 those runs are historical evidence, not
receipts for the canonical profile. On 2026-11-09, `_≤_` and the
constructor-injectivity lemmas were restated so that the core checks under
`--cubical-compatible` with no `UnsupportedIndexedMatch` warning. The
statements of the theorems below did not change.
* *Expected rejections.* Eight claims must be refused, each with the message
its `-- EXPECT:` line names. In order, those messages are:
`min m n != max m n`, `p != zero`, `suc _ != zero`, `max m n != m + n`,
`max p₁ (n₁ + p₂) != p₁ + p₂`, `3 != 5`, `inf != fin 3` and `… != bot`.
These are controls for the PROVEN rows. They are not themselves theorems.

== Morphisms (Morphism.agda)

A `Hom A B` preserves 𝟘, 𝟙, ⊕ and ⊗: that is what transporting a grade
equality needs. A `LaxHom A B` preserves 𝟙 and ⊕, so it is monotone, and
it maps ⊗ to at most ⊗: that is what transporting a bound needs.

[cols="1,4,2,1",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Claim | Agda (§5) | pwg

| F-1
| Both notions have identities and compose, and every strict morphism is
lax.
| `PROVEN` (`id-hom`, `_∘h_`, `id-lax`, `_∘l_`, `lax`)
| ungraded

| F-2
| `peak : HWM → MaxPlus` is lax: the peak of a sequence never exceeds the
sum of the peaks.
| `PROVEN` (`peak-lax`)
| ungraded

| F-3

units.
| `PROVEN` (`peak-not-strict`); control `tests/reject/PeakIsStrict.agda`
| ungraded

| F-4
| No strict morphism leaves HWM for an algebra with 𝟘 ≢ 𝟙. In particular,
none goes to MaxPlus or to Loss.
| `PROVEN` (`no-strict-from-HWM`, `no-strict-HWM→MaxPlus`,
`no-strict-HWM→Loss`)
| ungraded

| F-5

`support`. Neither distinguishes `fin 1` from `fin 2`.
| `PROVEN`. Both are morphisms (`zero-test-hom`, `support-hom`) and every

(`maps-disagree-at-positive`, tests/unit/MorphismTest.agda), and every
morphism collapses `fin 1` and `fin 2` (`Loss→MaxPlus-collapses`,
`no-injective-Loss→MaxPlus`).
| ungraded

| `PROVEN` (`support′-hom`, `MaxPlus→Loss`)
| ungraded
