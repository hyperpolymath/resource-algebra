== Morphisms (Morphism.agda)

`Hom A B` preserves 𝟘, 𝟙, ⊕ and ⊗ (what transporting a grade equality
needs). `LaxHom A B` preserves 𝟙 and ⊕, hence is monotone, and maps ⊗ to at
most ⊗ (what transporting a bound needs).

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]

| ID | Claim | Status

| F-2 | `peak : HWM → MaxPlus` is lax: the peak of a sequence never exceeds the sum of the peaks | PROVEN (`peak-lax`)
| F-3 | `peak` is not strict: it does not preserve ⊗, whatever the units do | PROVEN (`peak-not-strict`); control `tests/reject/PeakIsStrict.agda`

| F-5 | Exactly two strict morphisms Loss → MaxPlus exist, `zero-test` and `support`, and neither distinguishes `fin 1` from `fin 2` | PROVEN (`zero-test-hom`, `support-hom`, `Loss→MaxPlus`, `Loss→MaxPlus-collapses`, `no-injective-Loss→MaxPlus`)

== Proof Witness Grades (PROOF-POLICY §8)

Every theorem in this repository is *theory* (PROOF-POLICY §1): Agda is the
source, and §2 owes a Rocq recheck and a Lean recheck for each.

* *Agda leg.* Route: source. Invocation: `scripts/check-agda.sh`, i.e.
  `agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible --no-libraries
  --ignore-interfaces --double-check -i src`, on Agda 2.6.4.3 (Debian
  `agda-bin=2.6.4.3-1+b2`, asserted by `proofs.yml`). No standard library.
  Import cone: `src/ResourceAlgebra/` only (`All.agda` and the modules it
  imports). Axioms (§8.4.6): `--safe` refuses `postulate`, and
  `scripts/check-agda.sh` also scans the cone for `postulate` outside
  comments, so the postulate count is 0. The receipt is the `proofs.yml` run
  on the commit in question.
* *Rocq leg.* OPEN for every theorem: not started; no Rocq statement
  exists. §2.2 state: AGDA-VERIFIED on the Agda side at a commit with the
  receipt above, AGDA-PENDING otherwise.
* *Lean leg.* OPEN for every theorem: not started. M-2 is the one Lean
  artefact in view (tropical-types' `CommSemiring Tropical`); it is
  target-only evidence for MaxPlus, BRIDGE-PENDING, and is not this
  repository's Lean leg (§3).

Grade, derived as §8.4 requires: each PROVEN row is `PWG-3` at a commit whose
`proofs.yml` run passed the invocation above, and `ungraded` at any commit
without that receipt. Runs before 2026-11-09 used `--safe --without-K`

receipts. No theorem here is `PWG-2i` or better, and none is
