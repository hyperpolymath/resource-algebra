= Proof Requirements — resource-algebra
:revdate: 2026-11-18

What this repository owes, classified by the estate proof policy
(`3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc` in `hyperpolymath/standards`). What has been
discharged, and how, is in `PROOF-STATUS.adoc`.

== Classification

Every obligation here is *theory* (policy §1): the algebra, its instances,
the morphisms between them and the separations hold for any implementation.
The repository ships no executable code, ABI, FFI, wire format or protocol.

[cols="2,1,3",options="header"]

[[pn-7]]
=== PN-7 ruling and route (owner, 2026-11-18)

*Ruling.* The Agda and Lean legs earn `:image` through our own prover images,
published on GHCR and pinned by digest, with the checker baked in. The images
live in a shared estate repository, provisionally named `prover-images` (the
name is not yet checked against `gv-clade-index`), not in this repository.
Record now, build later.

Draft handoff (untested, not built): `docs/handoff/prover-images/README.adoc`.

*Blocker.* The `prover-images` repository does not exist; the owner will
create it. Nothing has been built or published. The 28 rows stay PWG-2ii.

*Why not an upstream image.*

* Agda: no official or trusted image ships Agda 2.6.4.3. The candidates
(`ltchentw/agda:2.6.4.3`, `zyasserd/agda:2.6.4.3`) are individuals' images and
build a different binary from the Debian `agda-bin` this repository pins.
* Lean: no image carries 4.35.0. The `leanprovercommunity` images stop at
2024-08 and GHCR has no `leanprover` images.
* Also rejected by the owner: a digest-pinned base with the install at run time
(not counted as isolated), and a Guix pack (an alternative path, noted, not
chosen).

*What closes it.* All four:

. the Agda and Lean images are published to GHCR;
. their digests are pinned in `proofs.yml` as `container: image: …@sha256:…`;
. each job summary prints the image digest the checker ran in;
. a new receipt is recorded from a run on the merge commit.

== Banned constructs

No escape hatch may appear in a proof file: `postulate` (Agda, refused by
`--safe`), `Admitted`/`admit`/`Axiom` without a recorded disposition (Rocq),
`sorry`/`axiom` without a recorded disposition (Lean). `just
proof-scan-dangerous` and CI's `panic-attack assail` enforce this.
