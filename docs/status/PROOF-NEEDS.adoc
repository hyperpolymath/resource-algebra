= Proof Requirements — resource-algebra
:revdate: 2026-11-09

What this repository owes, classified by the estate proof policy
(`3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc` in `hyperpolymath/standards`). What has been
discharged, and how, is in `PROOF-STATUS.adoc`.

== Classification

Every obligation here is *theory* (policy §1): the algebra, its instances,
the morphisms between them and the separations hold for any implementation.
The repository ships no executable code, ABI, FFI, wire format or protocol.

[cols="2,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Obligation class | Applies? | Why

| Theory (Agda, rechecked in Rocq and Lean)
| Yes
| Everything in `src/ResourceAlgebra/`.

| APIs, ABIs and engineering properties (Idris2)
| No
| No exported functions, memory layout or foreign interface exist to state
them about. The template's mandatory ABI rows (ABI-1..5) and its typing
rows (TP-1, TP-2) named template example files, not this repository's code;
they were removed with those files on 2026-11-09.

| Rust (Creusot, Pearlite, Verus) and Ada (SPARK)
| No
| No Rust or Ada code.

| Protocols (TLA+, policy §4 exception)
| No
| No concurrent or distributed behaviour.
|===

== Obligations

[cols="1,4,2",options="header"]
|===
| # | Obligation | Where

| PN-1
| Every theorem in `PROOF-STATUS.adoc` checks in Agda under the canonical
invocation `--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible` (policy §2.1), with no
postulates, holes, libraries or warnings.
| `scripts/check-agda.sh`; CI `proofs.yml`

| PN-2
| Each theorem is rechecked in Rocq. Route: Dedukti transport
(Agda2Dedukti → Lambdapi → Rocq) first, hand re-proof where transport fails.
The statement correspondence is recorded per theorem (policy §2.2).
| `verification/proofs/coq/`

| PN-3
| Each theorem is rechecked in Lean, re-proved by hand without Mathlib (no
released exporter reaches Lean; policy §2). The statement correspondence is
recorded per theorem.
| `verification/proofs/lean4/`

| PN-4
| Every PROVEN row cites the declaration that discharges it, and each
expected rejection fails with its stated message.
| `tests/reject/`, `scripts/check-agda.sh`

| PN-5
| Close or keep stating the open theory rows: C-4b (an annihilating ordered
algebra that is not a dioid) and M-2 (agreement with tropical-types' Lean
semiring).
| `PROOF-STATUS.adoc`
|===

== Out of scope here

* K-CUT-LOSS, and its transport along a morphism, belong to
`choreographic-types` (this repository's IS-NOT). This repository supplies
the algebra it is parametric in.
* occupancy-types' own algebra: HWM is a proposal to it (H-2).

== Banned constructs

No escape hatch may appear in a proof file: `postulate` (Agda, refused by
`--safe`), `Admitted`/`admit`/`Axiom` without a recorded disposition (Rocq),
`sorry`/`axiom` without a recorded disposition (Lean). `just
proof-scan-dangerous` and CI's `panic-attack assail` enforce this.
